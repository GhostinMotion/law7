    Write like it's paper.

    Pen, finger or trackpad. On a tablet it ignores your palm, so you can rest your hand like you do on paper. No keyboard, no stopping to type out your sums.

    Circle what you don't get.

    Draw a box around the exact line you're stuck on and press Ask. Atlas only looks inside the box, so the answer is about that one bit and not your whole page.

    • Ask about a box
    • or the whole page
    • Type it, or say it

    Atlas reads your handwriting.

    It reads your pen, even when it's messy. If one word is too scribbled it tells you which one instead of guessing, and numbers like atomic masses are looked up, never made up.

    • Reads your pen
    • Says what it can't read
    • No invented numbers

    It finds your mistake. It doesn't just hand you the answer.

    Show your working and ask if it's right. Atlas finds the first line that goes wrong, tells you why, and lets you fix it yourself. Want the full solution? Just ask. You choose how it teaches.

    • Hints first
    • Short answers
    • Exam tips
    • Plain words
    • Easier ways, checked

    Atlas draws it for you.

    Ask for a flowchart, a graph, a force diagram or a cell. Atlas draws it by hand on your page next to your notes, then checks that nothing overlaps. It can write out a whole worked solution too, step by step.

      Why I built this.

      Hi, I'm Ghost.

      I'm a high-school student. The night before a test I'm usually alone with a worksheet, a pen and one question I can't get past.

      Asking a chatbot meant leaving my notes, typing the problem out and copying the answer back. Half the time I copied it without understanding it.

      So I built what I wanted: a notebook where I write by hand, circle the bit I'm stuck on, and Atlas answers right there on the page. It points at my mistake instead of just handing me the answer.

      I built it for me. Now I want to see if it helps you too. That's what the test is for. If something confuses you or breaks, tell me. I read everything.

      Ghost

      Questions

      Does it cost anything?

      Testing is free.

      What do I need?

      A modern browser. A tablet with a pen is the best fit, but a finger, a mouse or a laptop trackpad also work.

      Who can see my boards?

      Your boards are saved under your own Claude.ai account and other testers can't open them.

      Why do you need my Claude.ai email?

      Law7 runs inside Claude.ai. I invite you with the email your Claude account uses, and Claude.ai asks you to accept when you open the link. If you use a different email than your Claude account, the invitation will not work on your account.

      Will Atlas just do my homework?

      It shows its steps on your page so you can follow them. How much you ask for is up to you. I'd say use it when you're stuck, not instead of trying first.

      Why only 10 people?

      I'm paying for the Atlas questions myself, and a small group lets me fix things fast and keeps everyone's daily allowance generous.

      How do I leave the test?

      Stop using it whenever you like. If you want your access closed, message me and I'll do it.
