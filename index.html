    Everything a study session needs, and nothing that gets in the way.

    Write the way you already do.

    Pen, finger or trackpad. Hold a line, circle or box still and it snaps clean. There's a highlighter too, and if you scribble over a mistake it just disappears.

    no typing needed

    Circle it, then ask.

    Draw a box around the thing you're stuck on and press Ask. Claude only looks at what's inside, so it answers about that bit and not your whole page.

    ask about just that bit

    Answers land on your page.

    Claude writes its steps right next to your working, in its own colour. Keep what helps, ignore the rest. It's one page, not a chat log you have to scroll back through.

    your page, not a chat log

    Bring your own pages.

    Open a PDF, paste a screenshot, add a photo of a worksheet, or start blank. I keep one folder per subject so chemistry doesn't end up under Arabic.

    worksheets welcome

    Claude draws, too.

    Ask for a flowchart, a force diagram or a cell and Claude draws it next to your work, then checks nothing overlaps. It can write a whole solution out step by step too.

    diagrams on your page

    Take it offline.

    Pick the pages you want, hit Export, and get one PDF with your writing on it and a bookmark for every page. For when the Wi-Fi dies the night before the test.

    your notes, anywhere

    Questions

    Does it cost anything?

    Testing is free.

    What do I need?

    A modern browser. A tablet with a pen is the best fit, but a finger, a mouse or a laptop trackpad also work.

    Who can see my boards?

    Your boards are saved under your own code and other testers can't open them.

    Will Claude just do my homework?

    It shows its steps on your page so you can follow them. How much you ask for is up to you. I'd say use it when you're stuck, not instead of trying first.

    Why only 10 people?

    I'm paying for the Claude questions myself, and a small group lets me fix things fast and keeps everyone's daily allowance generous.

    How do I leave the test?

    Stop using it whenever you like. If you want your code closed, message me and I'll do it.
