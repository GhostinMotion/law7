    Everything a study session needs, and nothing that gets in the way.

    Write the way you already do.

    Pen, finger or trackpad. Hold a line for three seconds and it snaps straight. Scribble over a mistake and it disappears.

    no typing needed

    Circle it, then ask.

    Use the Ask box on anything: a line of working, a diagram, a whole question. Claude looks at that part of your page and answers about it.

    ask about just that bit

    Answers land on your page.

    Claude writes its steps next to your working, so you can keep what helps and ignore the rest. Your notes and its answers stay in one place.

    your page, not a chat log

    Bring your own pages.

    Open a PDF, paste a screenshot, add a photo of a worksheet, or start blank. Folders keep each subject apart.

    worksheets welcome

    Questions

    Does it cost anything?

    Testing is free.

    What do I need?

    A modern browser. A tablet with a pen is the best fit, but a finger, a mouse or a laptop trackpad also work.

    Who can see my boards?

    Your boards are saved under your own code and aren't shared with other testers.

    Will Claude just do my homework?

    It's built to show its steps on your page so you can follow them. You decide how much to ask for.

    Why only 10 people?

    A small group lets us fix what's confusing quickly, and keeps everyone's daily allowance generous.

    How do I leave the test?

    Stop using it whenever you like. If you want your code closed, tell us and we'll do it.
